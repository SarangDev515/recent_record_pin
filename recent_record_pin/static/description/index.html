Quick Record Pin & History Sidebar

Your important records, one click away.

Return to your ten most recently viewed forms and pin the records you use most often. Access everything from a compact thumb-tack menu in the Odoo top navigation bar.

What the module does

Automatic recent history

Opening a saved record form adds it to Recently Viewed. The newest record appears first. The list holds up to ten unique records; when full, the oldest entry is removed. Revisiting a record moves it to the top instead of creating a duplicate.

Independent pinned shortcuts

Pin important entries from the recent list. They appear in Pinned Records and remain there even when they fall out of recent history. When a pinned record is revisited, its saved display name is refreshed.

Native form navigation

Click a record name to open that exact record in a form using Odoo's native action service. Normal access checks and unsaved-change navigation guards continue to apply.

Simple, polished controls

A larger 20px navbar pin, a pinned-count badge, subtle hover states, keyboard-focus outlines, truncated long labels and compact action buttons keep the dropdown easy to scan. Motion is reduced when the browser requests reduced animation.

How to use it

  1. Open a saved record. Visit a contact, sales order, invoice or another form using the standard Odoo form controller. The record is added automatically.
  2. Open the thumb-tack menu. Click the pin icon in the top navbar to see Pinned Records and Recently Viewed.
  3. Pin an important entry. Click its pin button in Recently Viewed. The navbar badge reflects the total number of pins.
  4. Return instantly. Click any record name to open its form. Pinning and removing shortcuts keep the menu open; selecting a record closes it.
  5. Keep your shortcuts tidy. Use a trash button to remove an entry from its section. Use the pin button again in Recently Viewed to unpin an already pinned record.
Example workflow: Open a customer, then a quotation, then an invoice. Pin the customer and quotation for regular access. Continue working: the recent list changes, while your pins remain available until you remove them.

Remove shortcuts safely

ControlResult
Trash in Pinned RecordsRemoves the pin only. Any existing recent-history entry stays.
Trash in Recently ViewedRemoves the recent entry only. Any existing pin stays.
Pin button on a pinned recent entryUnpins the record without removing its recent entry.
Reopen a removed recent entryAdds the record back to recent history automatically.

Removing a shortcut never deletes an actual Odoo record. Removal is immediate and does not require a confirmation dialog.

Tracking behavior

  • Tracks successfully rendered, saved form records rather than every requested action.
  • Handles record changes through the form pager and forms opened in dialogs.
  • Ignores new, unsaved records; tracking starts after the record receives a valid saved ID.
  • Identifies entries by model and record ID, so equal IDs in different models remain separate.
  • Uses the loaded display name without an extra record-name request; missing names fall back to model #ID.
  • List and kanban browsing alone does not add entries. Custom interfaces that do not use the standard form controller may not be tracked.

Installation and updates

  1. Place the recent_record_pin folder in an Odoo addons directory.
  2. Ensure that directory is included in the server's addons_path, then restart Odoo.
  3. Enable developer mode and select Update Apps List.
  4. Remove the default Apps filter if necessary. Search for Quick Record Pin & History Sidebar and install it.
  5. Refresh the web client and look for the thumb-tack icon in the top navbar.

After changing module files: upgrade the module and hard-refresh the browser. Developer asset mode (?debug=assets) can help diagnose cached assets.

Persistence, privacy and limitations

Shortcuts are stored in browser localStorage, with separate buckets for each Odoo database and user. The two keys are odoo_recent_records and odoo_pinned_records. Each entry contains only a model name, record ID and display name.

  • Browser-local, not server-synced: history and pins normally survive refresh in the same browser profile and site, but do not automatically follow you to another browser or device.
  • Not encrypted: anyone with access to this browser's storage may inspect saved labels and identifiers. Account buckets prevent mixed lists in the interface, not local storage inspection.
  • Site-data clearing: clearing the browser's site data removes shortcuts. Uninstalling the addon does not automatically erase stored data.
  • Storage restrictions: unavailable or full storage can limit persistence. Write failures show a warning and preserve the current in-memory list; Odoo may fall back to RAM storage when localStorage is unavailable.
  • Multiple tabs: storage-change events refresh affected lists. Simultaneous edits are last-write-wins rather than transactional merges.
  • Permissions still apply: pins do not grant access. Deleted or inaccessible records may remain as stale shortcuts; remove them from the menu if they cannot be opened.
  • Company context: buckets are separated by database and user, not by company. Navigation uses the current Odoo company context and access rules.
  • Older unscoped storage: legacy arrays are not imported automatically because their database/user ownership cannot be established.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the recent list empty?

Open a saved record in a standard form view. Simply browsing a list, or opening a new unsaved form, does not create a history entry.

Will a pinned record disappear after ten other records?

No. The ten-entry limit applies only to recent history. Pins are maintained independently.

Can I remove a record from both sections?

Yes. Remove its pin and its recent entry separately. The backend record is untouched.

Why did a removed recent entry come back?

Opening that record again adds it to history. Removal clears the shortcut; it does not block future tracking.

Does this record every kind of activity?

No. This is a saved-form navigation aid, not an audit log. It does not record field changes, list browsing, every user action or server-side activity.

Is this a permanently docked sidebar?

No. Despite the module name, the current implementation is a navbar systray dropdown containing pinned and recent sections.
